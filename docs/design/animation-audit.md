# Animation audit (0.8.14)

Every animation of the app on `main` after 0.8.13, checked against the four motion rules of [ADR 0055](../adr/0055-feel-of-0-8-14.md) (Feel of 0.8.14, the ADR spec #886 calls 0054):

- **Timing:** 200–300 ms.
- **Gestures:** what follows the finger moves with a spring, not a timed curve.
- **UI thread:** Reanimated on the UI thread.
- **Reduce Motion:** nothing moves across the screen; an opacity fade, or nothing where there is nothing to fade.

The audit reads the code of `packages/ui` and `apps/mobile` (Reanimated, gesture-handler, timers and haptics). It changes no code: each area then gets one fix ticket, which builds the proposed changes below and updates the motion table of [the design system](design-system.md#motion-sound-and-haptics) to match. "Reduce Motion" below is what `useReduceMotion()` gates; every timed animation passes `ReduceMotion.Never`, so the hook alone decides.

Status: ✅ follows the rules, ⚠️ breaks one, — not an animation.

## Summary of the fixes

One fix per area, as ADR 0055 asks:

- **Card transition:** the swipe settles on a velocity spring (C1); turns without a swipe take 300 ms (C2).
- **Heart:** the baseline spring to 1.2 and back in 300 ms (H1).
- **Game:** the shake (G2), the Life's pop (G4), the progress bar (G5, set at once under Reduce Motion) and the win rosette (G6) fit in 300 ms.
- **Sheets:** 300 ms in (S1); a critically damped spring for the snap back and the drag close, at once under Reduce Motion for the snap back (S4, S5).
- **Other:** the loading divider moves to the UI thread (O1); the onboarding step (O5) and the widget guide (O6) take 300 ms, the onboarding step gated by the hook.

## Questions for the owner

- **Loops.** The loading divider (O1) and the skeleton pulse (O2) are loops, not one-off moves. The audit reads the 200–300 ms rule as one for one-off animations and keeps the loops' design timing (140 ms a stitch, 1.4 s a pulse).
- **The right answer.** The audit keeps the green fill until «Далі» instead of a brief flash (see the baseline table).
- **The snap back under Reduce Motion (S4).** The audit returns the dragged sheet at once; a 200 ms fade cannot return it, since the sheet is already moved.

## Owner checks

Cloud sessions have no simulator, so the audit was read from the code. On the SLOVO iPhone 16-class and SLOVO-SE simulators, before the fix tickets:

- Watch each ⚠️ finding with Reduce Motion off and on, and confirm the timing reads as described.
- Swipe the Feed slowly and fast, open and drag-close a sheet, fill the heart, answer right and wrong in each answer form, lose a Life and win a Game.
- Take the screenshots the ticket asks for: the Feed card mid-swipe, a sheet mid-slide, the heart at its peak, a wrong answer mid-shake, on both simulators.
