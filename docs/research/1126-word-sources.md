# Word sources: where the next thousands of rare Words come from

Question: `pnpm source:lemmas` (ADR 0196) feels exhausted after about 20k Lemmas looked at, and `pnpm source:words` fails on 50–60% of rare Lemmas (no entry, cross-reference, missing data, dialect without Region). Which sources give the next thousands of Words, and how do we add them to `tools/sourcing` within ADR 0146, 0094 and 0196?

Checked on 2026-11-26 from a cloud session. `robots.txt` files were fetched that day. Nothing was written to the backend; the content snapshot was only read. Counts marked _measured_ were computed in this session; the rest come from the sources' own pages.

## Verdict

1. **The headword pool is not empty; it is diluted.** The pinned list still holds about 218k candidates that are no Word of the Edition (rarity 2: 8.7k, 3: 10.0k, 4: 14.4k, 5: 184.6k, _measured_). Rarity 5 is mostly what dict_uk (ВЕСУМ) generates and no explanatory dictionary lists: verbal nouns in `-ння`, `-ість` nouns, prefixed and `-ся` verbs, `взаємо-`/`енерго-`/`макро-` compounds, relative adjectives of place names. A random sample of 60 rarity-5 Lemmas gave about 8 a writer would keep (скніти, опука, скажениця, вибалушити, пронирливий, опояска, духовниця, смеркати). That, not a lack of headwords, is why `source:words` fails on half the list. The cheapest thousands come from **picking better and following references**, not from a new dictionary.
2. **Грінченко (1907–09, public domain, ~67k headwords) is the one big new source** that the Rare Word rule (`CONTEXT.md`) already accepts as a normative dictionary. It holds exactly the old, folk and dialect words SLOVO wants, many of which СУМ-11 lacks. The catch is access: the sites that carry it forbid our crawler, so the tool needs its own copy of a public-domain scan.
3. **Region is now solvable for several dialect areas.** archive.org has OCR text of at least ten dialect dictionaries (Bukovyna, Polissia, Boikivshchyna, Lemko, Hutsul, East Slobozhanshchyna, Odesa, Transcarpathia). Used as a membership check (the fact "dictionary Z records word X in region Y"), they turn a `region-missing` draft into a dialect Word. This reopens the owner's 2026-11-06 choice not to draft dialect Words (`docs/research/dialect-regions.md`).
4. **Wiktionary is clean but small**: about 1.2k labelled Ukrainian lemmas that are no Word yet, almost all already in our headword list. Use its labels to rank, not as a source of Words or text.

## (2) Sources that fill the no-entry gap

Expected yield: the dialect leads are the `arch`-tagged Lemmas, goroh's «діал.» articles and the ~425 Wiktionary `dialectal` lemmas. A Lemma found in one of these dictionaries, with the sense matched by a human on the page, gives a dialect Word: **0.5–1.5k** across the areas above. OCR noise (stress shown as capitals or digits, soft hyphens) means every match stays a lead until the human check, as `dialect-regions.md` already says.

## Recommended plan

**Step 1. Fix the funnel we have (~1.5–2.5k Words; small effort).**

- `source:lemmas`: drop productive derivatives before they reach a writer (verbal nouns in `-ння`/`-іння` whose verb is a Word or common, `-ість` of a common adjective, `взаємо-`/`енерго-`/`макро-`/`міні-`/`соц-` compounds, adjectives in `-ський` of place names, adverbs in `-о`/`-но` of a common adjective, `-ся` pairs) and rank the rest by evidence: tagged `arch`, labelled by Wiktionary (`dated`, `rare`, `archaic`, `obsolete`, `dialectal`), present in goroh's ЕСУМ. Strike old-orthography variants (`alt`, and `arch` Lemmas that differ from a Word only by ґ/е/і spelling).
- `source:words`: follow a cross-reference one step. If the target is rare and no Word, the target becomes the draft; if it is common, the Lemma is dropped with the reason. When СУМ has no article, give the writer ЕСУМ's gloss as the meaning basis, still requiring a normative dictionary.
- Drop `sum.in.ua` as a default fallback (it has been down at every check).
- Expected: the share of a list that reaches `drafts/` goes from 40–50% to 70%+; on ~4k useful Lemmas left in rarity 2–4 and the evidence-ranked part of rarity 5, that is 1.5–2.5k Words.
- **ADR**: amend 0196: the pick ranks by evidence and leaves out productive derivatives; Wiktionary labels (CC BY-SA, used as facts only, no text) may rank candidates, pinned by dump date and SHA-256 like the other two files. The cross-reference step and the ЕСУМ basis are tool rules: README only, no ADR.

**Step 2. Грінченко as a headword and proof source (~3–5k Words; large effort).**

- Build a headword file from a public-domain scan: the 1927–28 DjVu from r2u's download page (r2u's robots allow it; only `/s` lookups are disallowed) or an archive.org copy of 1907–09, run our own OCR, keep `<headword>\t<gloss>\t<sigla>` in the sourcing cache, pinned by SHA-256, never committed (as ADR 0196 does for its files). No scraping of hrinchenko.com or r2u lookups; no use of the AGPL GitHub conversion.
- `source:lemmas` takes Грінченко headwords that are no Word, spelled in today's orthography and still alive in the modern headword list or in ЕСУМ; `source:words` drafts with goroh when СУМ has the Word, and otherwise from Грінченко: the Russian gloss and the source sigla as the writer's basis, a folklore Example as an Example lead, flagged so the writer knows the Definition must be written fresh.
- Pilot first: 200 Lemmas, measure the yield, then a wave.
- **ADR**: a new ADR "Грінченко is a Word source": the PD text is read from our own copy of a scan; membership in Грінченко proves a Rare Word (the glossary already allows it); its Russian gloss is a basis, never quoted; its folklore Examples count as public domain under ADR 0146. It amends 0196 (a second headword list) and the `source:words` rule that drafts come only from explanatory dictionaries. Желехівський can join the same ADR later as a second PD dictionary, once the owner accepts it as a proving dictionary.

**Step 3. Region from dialect dictionaries (~0.5–1.5k dialect Words; medium effort, owner decision first).**

- A `regions` step in `source:words`: for a `region-missing` draft, search the OCR texts of the dictionaries in (3), downloaded once into the cache and pinned; on a match, propose the Region from the dictionary's title or its front-matter key of sigla, and attach the page link. A human compares the senses on the page scan before the owner's yes (ADR 0146).
- `source:lemmas` stops leaving out dialect leads once a Region source exists.
- **ADR**: a new ADR reopening the 2026-11-06 choice: a dialect Word's Region comes from a listed dialect dictionary as a fact with its citation (title, page), the dictionary's text is never quoted, and a match is a lead until a human checks the sense; it names the dictionaries allowed. It amends 0196 on `dial` and 0146 on the Region of a dialect Word.

Together: roughly **5–9k new Words**, against 7,655 today, with Step 1 alone able to start the next wave.

## Caveats

- Yields are estimates except where marked _measured_; each step should start with a 200-Lemma pilot.
- Licences: only public-domain texts (Грінченко, Желехівський) and CC BY-SA / CC BY data are ingested in bulk. Every modern dictionary (СУМ-11, СУМ-20, ВТССУМ, ЕСУМ, the dialect dictionaries) is in copyright and serves as a reference or a fact, as today.
- The 2022 database right in Ukraine is a reason never to bulk-copy a site's structured data even when the underlying text is public domain.
- Not verified page by page: the Грінченко source sigla to Region mapping, and whether an archive.org copy of the 1907–09 edition has a usable text layer.
- No handbook page changes: this is research, no feature changed.
