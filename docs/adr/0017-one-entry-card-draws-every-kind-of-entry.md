# One Entry card draws every kind of Entry; the Edition's words and voice come from a provider

The UI draws every Entry, a Word, a Correction or an Idiom (ADR 0046), with one Entry card. It takes the Core's card model and draws it in one frame: the compact layout of narrow screens, the same corner ornaments (Decor slot 3, ADR 0014) for every kind, or a plain card when the Edition has none, and a slot for the action row under it. A screen never assembles a card from parts, so the Feed and the Saved detail show the same card and each action is added in one place.

What the card needs from the Edition comes from an `EditionProvider`, as the tokens, the Decor and the font size do: the UI strings, the language VoiceOver reads in and the Pronunciation button. The Category name is not among them: it comes with the card model, from the Language Pack (ADR 0033). The button is the one part the shell supplies as a component, because it speaks through the device's voice, which the UI package does not reach. The shell connects it once, at the root.

**Self-check** belongs to the card. A screen tells it only whether Self-check applies there. The card keeps the reveal, decides what each kind conceals until it (a Word's Definition and what follows, and its register labels; a Correction's right form, explanation and type; everything after an Idiom's divider), conceals it again once the card leaves the screen, and tells its action slot through a React context, so the Knowledge Mark buttons there stay inactive until the reveal. The action row stays the shell's. «Поділитися» and «До колекції» stay active while the card is concealed, though the Share Card shows the meaning (owner, 21.10.2026).

**The Idiom card** draws the Core's Idiom card model and picks no sections itself: the expression at the Lemma's size with the chip of its Idiom Type under it; the ornament divider; the primary Definition and the others numbered on from 2 under «Інші значення»; every Example; «Походження» with the etymology; and the line naming the sources. A place name or a proverb differs only in its chip. The expression and its chip are one VoiceOver header (expression, then kind, through the Edition's heading string). The expression has no Stress, so there is no Pronunciation button. Under Self-check it keeps the expression and its chip and reveals the rest with the Word card's button. It shows all its sections at once, without the Word card's «Детальніше»: an Idiom has fewer of them, and the story of a place name is the point of its card.

**One card shape.** The Core's card of an Entry has the same fields for every kind: its kind, `entryId` and `model` (the Word, Correction or Idiom card model, which narrows by the kind), plus the Word of the Day's day and the Review label. Screens and the Feed read the id and the model directly; only drawing the card's body branches on the kind. The end-of-Cycle card shows no Entry, so its `entryId` is undefined. The card models keep their own `wordId`, `correctionId` and `idiomId`, which the Game, the Entry rows and the card bodies read; renaming them would touch every one of those tests without removing a branch.

**Round models.** The Core builds the Game's Round models as it builds the card models, and the Round screen (`GameRound`) draws a finished model, deciding nothing of its form. A Round carries its form resolved when it is made: `gap`, the highlight that stands for the Lemma in an Example, which the screen labels with the Edition's gap label and VoiceOver reads as a gap instead of naming it as a highlight; each blank of a typed Round as a mark that stays in place (an apostrophe of any kind or a hyphen), a letter «Відкрити літеру» opened, or an empty blank; and `lifeLostAfter`, when a wrong answer costs its Life, which is `LIFE_LOST_DELAY`, the moment of the Life's haptic (ADR 0025), so the screen pops the Life then. The screen imports no decision helper from the Core, so a Jest test renders it over a given model and asserts only what is drawn, and each rule is tested once, in Vitest, through the Round the Game hands out.

**Share Card content.** The Core builds what a Share Card shows (`buildShareCardContent`), and `ShareCard` draws it, deciding nothing of its text. From an Entry's card (with whether it is today's Word of the Day) or a Weekly Recap, in the Edition's strings, it gives one `ShareCardContent` for every kind: what it shows (the Entry's id or the week, so a new one is measured afresh), the overline (today's Word of the Day or the Category, the Correction Type, the chip of the Idiom Type, of a row or of a Tip, or the week), what VoiceOver reads, its Stress marks removed, and the body by kind, each text final with its Stress where the card draws it: a Word's short grammar line, a place name's etymology alone, a Synonym Row's first 6 members ending in «…» when it has more (ADR 0051), a Weekly Recap's numbers without the Streak while the reader hides it (ADR 0054). The content is the same in both formats; the format only places the overline, atop the Story or in the Square's footer, and stays the card's. The Edition's fixed labels the card draws («Так часто кажуть», «Українською краще», «Походження») stay with the card, as on the Entry card. The text helpers the content needs (`stressLabel`, `wordGrammarText`, `sentences`) live in the Core with it, and the UI imports them from there.

**The Entry row** (`buildEntryRow` in the Core) names and reads an Entry wherever it is listed: Favorites, Collections, History, the Hidden screen, the «Вчу» list and the widget. For every kind it gives the title (a Word's Lemma with Stress, a Correction's main standard equivalent, an Idiom's expression), the second line (the primary Definition, or the non-standard form a Correction replaces), `labelKey` (`word`, `correction`, or the Idiom Type `idiom`, `placeName` or `proverb`, indexing the Edition's `history.kinds`) and the kind-specific VoiceOver text without the Stress mark. The widget reads the row's `spoken` as it stands (ADR 0026). Lists build their VoiceOver label from the Edition's words: `EntryRow` joins the title, the subtitle `entryRowSubtitle` makes (a Correction's «замість …») and the trailing text, and drops the Stress mark. One helper of the UI package (`@slovo/ui/entry-row-text`, `entryLabel`) turns the key into the Edition's word, so no screen branches on the kind for these texts and the Core stays language-agnostic. The Game Review is the one exception: it lists the Rounds just played, so a Correction's row leads with the non-standard form the Round asked about (`titleOf` in `GameReview.tsx`).

## Considered Options

- **A card per kind sharing a frame component**: rejected. Every screen would branch on the kind and pass each card its parts, and the action row would be added once per kind.
- **The Pronunciation button as a prop of the Entry card**: rejected. It is the same on every screen, so each screen would repeat it.
- **Pronunciation and its React binding moved into the UI package behind a port**: not adopted. It would move tested behaviour between packages for a single adapter.
- **The screen keeps the Self-check state and passes it to the card and the action row**: rejected. The two values had to agree with nothing to enforce it, and every screen with Self-check would repeat the wiring.
- **The Round screen resolves the form from the Core's helpers** (`gapOf`, `staysInPlace`, `LIFE_LOST_DELAY` imported into `GameRound`): rejected. Each decision crossed the seam twice, and the screen's logic could be tested only by rendering it.
- **The Share Card builds its own text from the card model** (`buildSynonymRowShareCard`, `buildTipShareCard` and `withoutStress` called in render, a branch per kind): rejected. The text was decided on both sides of the seam, and the card's logic could be tested only by rendering it.
- **A content per format**: not adopted. Only where the overline sits differs, and that is layout.
- **A render-prop action slot that receives the Self-check state**: not adopted. Only the Knowledge Mark buttons care, so a context keeps the slot a plain node.
