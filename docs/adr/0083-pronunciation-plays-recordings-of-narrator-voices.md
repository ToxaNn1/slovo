# Pronunciation plays Recordings of Narrator Voices, the device voice as fallback

Pronunciation was the device's voice for the Edition's language, which on many devices is missing or poor for Ukrainian. From spec #1633 Pronunciation plays a pre-generated Recording of a Word in the chosen Narrator Voice, and the device's voice reads a Word without one.

**Amended by ADR 0099.** Recordings live in the Media Store, not in the repo or the app: the mp3 files under `assets/recordings/`, the per-voice `texts.json`, the `recording-requests` command and the `Recordings` workflow are gone (#1770; the last commit with the files is `3c4770c8f`, from which `import-media` put them in the Media Store). An hourly Railway Cron on the Deployment makes the missing Recordings, and again those of a Word whose Lemma or Stress changed, so a new or changed Word gets its voice within the hour, through a Patch. The app bundles only the Starter Media and fetches the rest; the device voice reads a Word until its Recording is there. The bullets below on the repo folder, `texts.json`, the workflow, the JS assets and the build's Recording checks describe the decision before 0099.

- The Edition config declares its Narrator Voices: id, display name, description, and which is the default (UA: Оксана, Тарас, Марта, Остап; Оксана by default). The Core resolves the chosen voice; without a choice, or with one the Edition no longer declares, the default reads.
- Recordings are generated offline from ukrainian-tts v6.0.0, one mono 24 kHz 48 kbps mp3 per Word per voice, in `assets/recordings/<voice>/<Word id>.mp3` of the Edition. Only live Words are recorded: no Retired Word, no Corrections, Idioms or Synonym Row members. A voice reads a Word's Lemma with its first Stress marked: a Word with more than one valid Stress (UA: 16, such as за́грава/загра́ва) has one Recording, in its first Stress. Beside the mp3 files, `texts.json` in each voice folder maps every Word id to the text its Recording was made from. The mp3 files live in git as plain files, without LFS.
- Recordings are generated in the cloud, never on the owner's machine: the `Recordings` workflow (by hand or by a change of `tools/recordings/request.json`, on a branch, never `main`) lists the Words with `pnpm recording-requests <edition> --only all|missing`, the Lemma with its first Stress marked, runs `tools/recordings/generate.py` in the locked Python 3.10 environment on the model files of the upstream v6.0.0 release, each checked by SHA256, and commits the Recordings to that branch. Its Stress spot-check fails the run unless the engine stresses кромлех, антаблемент, кракелюр, заграва and заплава as marked. The workflow rebases on its branch before it pushes, so a branch that moved during the run keeps its Recordings. Engine voices: Оксана Tetiana, Тарас Mykyta, Марта Lada, Остап Oleksa; never Dmytro.
- All voices ship inside the app as JS assets (about 17 MB for 758 Words), the way Illustrations do (ADR 0055), not in the Language Pack (ADR 0032). A Content update carries no audio: its new Words fall back to the device voice until the next app update.
- The Edition build refuses a Recording of a Word id the Edition lacks, a folder of a voice it does not declare and a file that is not an mp3. It does not refuse a Word without a Recording; it reports the count per voice, counting as missing a Recording whose `texts.json` text differs from the Word's text now (a Stress or Lemma corrected since), so `--only missing` makes it again.
- The app plays a Recording through the slice of `expo-audio` the Interface sounds use (ADR 0070), behind the `audio` native-module guard: it does not play in iOS silent mode, mixes with the reader's music and never plays in the background. A binary without the module reads every Word with the device voice.
- The reader chooses the Narrator Voice in Settings › «Голос диктора», a row right after «Звуки», behind the `narratorVoice` Feature Switch, which needs `pronunciation`. The choice is the Settings record `settings.narratorVoice`, synced last write wins, by default the Edition's default voice. While the switch is off the row is gone and the default voice reads, whatever the record holds.
- The voice screen reads the Edition's sample Word (`narratorVoices.sample` in the Edition config: its Word id, syllables, stressed syllable and caption; UA: заплава) by its Recording, like a card's Word. A tap on a voice tile chooses it and reads the sample in it at once, from its start; the play button reads it or stops it. The sample stops when the screen closes. The Edition build refuses a sample Word id the Edition lacks. The sample Word has a single Stress, so its Recording stresses the syllable its caption shows.
- One Word is read at a time and a second tap stops it. With «Звуки» off nothing plays. `no-voice` shows only when a Word has no Recording and the device no voice for the language.

## Considered options

- **The device voice alone**: rejected. Many devices have no Ukrainian voice, and the ones that do misplace the Stress.
- **Recordings in the Language Pack**: rejected. It would multiply the size of every Content update by the audio of all voices.
- **Streaming Recordings from a server**: rejected. The app works offline, and the Edition has no audio server.
