# The quote check runs hourly over changed records

Owner defaults of 2026-11-08 (ticket #1721, spec #1708). ADR 0090 moves `pnpm content verify` (ADR 0084) into a background job on the Deployment; this records how. It amends ADR 0090 and supersedes ADR 0084 and 0080, whose live parts it now holds.

**Decision.** The job checks the quoted text of Words, Corrections, Idioms, Synonym Rows and Tips against their first source's page, never Literary Uses. A record is unverified while its record version has no check: every write other than an import raises it. Imported records count as checked, since CI verified them; a record with no source page is not checked and counts neither unverified nor failed. The job runs hourly (`0 * * * *`, UTC) as a Railway Cron service, `pnpm quote-check` on the backend image. Fetched pages stay in the Postgres sourcing cache for 30 days. A quote not found is flagged for the Content queue with the finding by the command `quote-check`; the record stays live. A page that cannot be fetched leaves the record for the next run.

**Consequences.** The matching lives in `@slovo/content-rules`, shared by `verify` and the backend. The admin content report carries `quoteCheck: {unverified, failed}`.

**Matching** (from ADR 0084). The check takes one quoted field per kind: a Word's or an Idiom's primary Definition, a Correction's explanation, a Synonym Row's meaning line, a Tip's explanation. Its first 40 characters, normalised, must be on the page the record's first source names, and on goroh in a block not attributed to «Велика мовна модель». `miss` and `llm` fail; `fetch-failed` is retried (above). A quotation is at most one sentence (ADR 0094).

**Sourcing drafters** (from ADR 0080 and 0084). `pnpm source:words` drafts Words, and `pnpm source:idioms`, `pnpm source:corrections` and `pnpm source:synonyms` draft Idioms, Corrections and Synonym Rows from goroh «Фразеологія», «Слововживання» and «Синонімія». Every Definition, Example and explanation is quoted word for word from the cited dictionary, never written by a model, and an article a source attributes to a language model is never used (ADR 0012). Only drafts a command marks ready are taken, never incomplete ones. Entries no command sources (Tips, Word Histories, Literary Uses, place-name Idioms) are quoted from their cited sources by agents. An agent adds no Entry whose text is already in the Edition and none without a source. A content wave runs by `docs/agents/content-wave.md` and `scripts/content-wave/launch.sh`.
