# The Daily Crossword is a crossing crossword played on a letter wheel

Owner decisions 07.11 (spec #1674, board 08): the traced grid of ADR 0073 gives way to a classic crossing crossword entered on a letter wheel. This ADR supersedes ADR 0073's Puzzle, Generation, Play, Progress and fallback; ADR 0073's card, XP, Share Card and «not a Game» stay, now stated here (ADR 0073 is superseded by this one). It amends ADR 0085 (one new Badge family shape) and ADR 0052 (the typed fallback goes). Behind `crossingCrossword` and its parts `crosswordHint`, `crosswordSolveTime`, `crosswordSolvedScreen` and `crosswordBadgeStamp` (needs `badges`); off, the traced crossword of ADR 0073 stays.

## Decision

- **The puzzle.** A 5×5 grid of blocks and white cells. Every horizontal or vertical run of two or more white cells is a Word. Words cross: a white cell may belong to one across and one down Word, and their letters agree. Every white cell belongs to at least one Word. A Word is a Word within the Rarity Ceiling (ADR 0072), Hidden Entries included as in ADR 0073, whose Lemma is lowercase letters of the Edition's alphabet with no space, hyphen or capital, at most once a day; its primary Definition is the clue. Cells are numbered in reading order where a Word starts.
- **Block patterns** are a fixed Core list of symmetric 5×5 patterns (board 08's four-block pattern among them), each giving 3–8 Words of lengths 3–5 and no run of exactly two cells.
- **Generation on the device.** A Core function takes the local date, the Words within the Rarity Ceiling, the Edition's alphabet and a random port, and draws only from that port. It seeds the port with the date and the ids of the eligible Words (as ADR 0073 does), picks a pattern, and fills it by a bounded backtracking search over the eligible Lemmas, trying the patterns in seeded order. It returns a puzzle or a typed `{ kind: 'no-fill' }` when no pattern fills within the search budget. Same date, same Rarity Ceiling and same Edition content give the same puzzle.
- **Pre-filled letters.** The generator pre-fills 2–3 crossing cells chosen by the seed, never every letter of a Word.
- **The wheel.** For the current Word: its letters (repeats kept as separate tiles) plus one decoy letter from the Edition's alphabet that is not in the Word, shuffled by the seed. The wheel is part of the puzzle, so it is the same for every reader.
- **Play.** One reducer over a state where each Word is a discriminated union `open | solved`, each cell `empty | prefilled | filled`, plus the current Word, the current wheel trace, a `hinted` flag and the elapsed on-screen seconds. Actions: select a cell (picks the Word through it; on a crossing a second tap switches direction), next Word (the next open Word in number order, wrapping), extend the trace onto a wheel tile not on it (onto the tile before the last steps back), step back, release (a trace that spells the current Word fills its cells and solves it; otherwise a miss, nothing changes), hint (fills the first empty cell of the current Word and sets `hinted`; a Word with no empty cell left is solved), tick (adds injected seconds while the screen is in front). A Word whose every cell is filled by crossings and hints is solved. Every Word solved solves the day; a solved puzzle takes no more actions. After a solve the current Word moves to the next open Word.
- **Progress.** The filled cells, the Words solved, `hinted`, the elapsed seconds, the date and the puzzle's fingerprint stay on the device; a new date or another puzzle starts empty; the trace is never kept. The time pauses when the screen leaves or the app goes to the background.
- **Sync of the result.** The XP log record of the Daily Crossword's solve gains the solve time in seconds and whether a hint was used. It syncs by id like every other record, so the solved card and the share line are the same on every device. A record from before reads as no time and no hint; the card then shows «✓ Розв’язано» with no time line. The XP stays 15, once a day (ADR 0066). The 7-day run (`dailyCrosswordRun`) reads solves as before, hints included.
- **Failure.** On `no-fill` the card is not shown that day. The typed crossword of ADR 0052 and its screen go; the Language Pack's recorded crossword days are no longer read by the app.
- **What stays from ADR 0073.** The Daily Crossword card, the Share Card and the XP stay: the solve appends the Daily Crossword's XP once for the date, in the same write (ADR 0066). The crossword is not a Game (ADR 0052): it makes nothing Seen and changes no Knowledge Mark. Hidden Entries stay in the pool, because the grid is a puzzle, not a surface that shows the Word anew.
- **Badge family shape.** ADR 0085's shapes gain `checker`: a tile with two cut corners (top-left, bottom-right), an ink frame, a cream inset and a 3×3 red and ink checker. The UA «Кросворд» family uses it.

- **In the contracts.** The `daily-crossword` XP log record carries `solveSeconds` and `hinted`, both optional; left out, they read as no time and no hint. A Badge may carry a `stampKicker`, the line over its name on the full-screen stamp, such as «7 ПОСПІЛЬ» on the UA `crossword-7`.

## Considered Options

- keep ADR 0073's traced grid (rejected by the owner: the board wins)
- recorded days in the Language Pack (rejected for the reason of ADR 0073: five Rarity Ceilings)
- a wheel of all the grid's letters (rejected: too many tiles)
- a hint that costs XP or breaks the run (rejected by the owner)
