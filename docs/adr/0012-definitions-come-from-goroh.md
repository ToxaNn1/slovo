# Ukrainian Definitions and Examples come from goroh «Тлумачення»

The UA Edition takes its Definitions and Examples from the «Тлумачення» pages of goroh.pp.ua, and only from the explanatory dictionary each page cites: СУМ-20 (vols 1–15, А to П'ЯТЬ) for the Lemmas it already covers, and СУМ-11 for the rest. The specs named СУМ-11 on sum.in.ua as the source. While the content was being sourced, sum.in.ua stopped answering, and the Wayback Machine started refusing this machine after parallel use, so neither could supply about 500 Words. The owner was told and agreed to the switch.

What this means for the content:

- Definitions and Examples are copied word for word from the cited dictionary as goroh prints it. The only edit is dropping the markers СУМ puts in front of an Example (`* У порівн.`, `* Образно.`).
- Each Word records the dictionary title exactly as goroh shows it, plus the goroh address. A Word may read «Тлумачний словник української мови. Томи 1-15» (СУМ-20) or «… в 11-ти томах» (СУМ-11).
- Articles goroh attributes to other dictionaries or to a language model are never used.
- sum.in.ua, or its Wayback Machine copy, stays the fallback for Lemmas goroh has no explanatory article for. A few Words come from there.
- goroh's text of СУМ-11 sometimes differs from the printed dictionary. For example, its сотник has the wrong centuries and a broken sentence. Words sourced from СУМ-11 through goroh have not been checked against sum.in.ua, because the site is still unreachable. When it is back, compare them, and retire and replace any Word whose goroh text is damaged (ADR 0010 keeps its id taken).

## Considered Options

- **Wait for sum.in.ua**: rejected. It blocked every content ticket for an unknown time.
- **Wayback Machine copies of sum.in.ua only**: rejected. Too few pages are archived, and the archive refuses this machine after a burst of requests.
- **Correct goroh's text by hand where it looks damaged**: rejected. That would be writing Definitions instead of quoting them. A damaged Word is retired and replaced instead.
