# One Entries module decides which Entries are eligible

The Core's Entries module is the only place that decides which Entries may appear anywhere new: the Feed, the Word of the Day, notifications, the widget and the Game. An Eligible Entry is live (not Retired), of a kind the Edition turns on and not off remotely (ADR 0040), in the reader's Mix and not Hidden; a Word must also meet the Rarity Floor, which Corrections and Idioms, having no rarity, ignore. Corrections and Idioms are each in or out of the Mix as a whole, each under a record of its own (`mix.corrections`, `mix.idioms`) that syncs last write wins (ADR 0041) and falls back to the Edition's default; a Correction must also have its Category of Corrections in the Mix (ADR 0050). Every other module asks the Entries and never checks Retired, a Feature Switch or the Mix itself. Any Entry stays reachable by id, Retired or switched off, so Favorites, Collections, History and the Knowledge Marks keep showing what the reader kept.

The eligible ids come as one list per kind (`EntryIds`), in the Language Pack's stable order, because the Cycle, the Feed's turns, the notification plan and the Game each take one kind at a time. A feature that weighs every eligible Entry of a kind at once, such as the Game planning its Rounds, gets either the Entries or why the kind as a whole is not eligible (Corrections, or Idioms, out of the Mix, whether or not the Edition turns them on), and passes that reason on (ADR 0024).

A change of eligibility applies at once, not from the next Cycle. A Cycle covers the Entries eligible when it opens or starts, and when the Mix or the Hidden Entries change it reads them again, keeping its seed and what is Seen. Each Entry's place in the Cycle is a hash of its id that starts from the seed, and the Cycle order is the Entries sorted by it, so the Entries that stay keep their order and an Entry eligible again gets its place back, also across a restart. Due Entries come back on top of that order in the Feed's Review slots (ADR 0043), only while they are eligible. No caller filters card by card.

**The content read port.** It lists the live ids of each kind (`listWordIds`, also for one Category, so the Mix costs one query per Category; `listCorrectionIds`, `listIdiomIds`), gives every live Word at once for the Game's Distractors (`listWords`), lists the Words a Correction links, and gives the Entries of any ids, whatever their kind and Retired ones included, in one call (`getEntries`). The pack reader asks its `entries` table which kind each id is, one query per 500 ids, then reads each kind present at once. The Entries module reads one Entry or many (`get`, `getMany`) through `getEntries`, so a list of kept Entries or a notification plan costs a few queries however many Entries it holds. The module reads the live Entries once per app session, since a pack never changes while it is open, and keeps every row it read; each call still filters by the eligible ids of that moment, and a read that fails is tried again on the next call. One contract suite holds both adapters, the in-memory one the Core ships for tests and the pack reader, to the same answers.

## Considered Options

- **Each feature checks the rules it needs**: rejected. Retired was checked in four places and the Corrections Feature Switch in three, and the Mix and Hidden would add two more rules to every feature.
- **The Core filters Retired Entries out of everything the port lists**: rejected. Listing ids would mean reading every Entry in full.
- **Eligibility changes apply from the next Cycle**: rejected. A Hidden Entry must never appear again, and a Cycle can last weeks.
- **A shuffle drawn from a generator, or the shuffled order kept in memory or in the user state**: rejected. A generator gives a new order as soon as one Entry leaves, a restart would shuffle again, and one record for the order is the one-blob shape ADR 0013 rejects; the order follows from the seed.
- **A kind-agnostic list of eligible ids, or one Mix record for Corrections and Idioms**: rejected. Every consumer takes one kind at a time, and each kind is a switch of its own on the Mix screen.
- **Per-kind getters by id, asked kind by kind**: rejected. The port would grow with every kind, and a pack that names each id's kind in one query would be read once per kind.
- **Read kept Entries from the live Words already in memory**: rejected. That read takes every Word in full, about 0.4 s, and the launch would wait for it for a single Favorite.
