# The Game asks about Words in History and draws Distractors from the whole pack

The Core has a Game: Rounds in one Game Mode, up to 15 Rounds with 5 Lives unless the mode sets its own (ADR 0052). The master spec says the Game asks about "Seen Entries", but Seen is reset by every new Cycle, so a reader who finished a Cycle would drop back to the whole Language Pack the next day.

**The answer pool** of a Game Mode is the eligible Entries it can ask about that are in History, when at least 15 of them make a Round in the mode; otherwise it is every eligible Entry it can ask about. It applies to every mode, with the limits each mode adds (ADR 0052). History survives Cycles and holds the 500 most recent Seen Entries, which is what "the Words I've met" means to the reader, and a new reader can play at once on the whole pack. Up to 5 Rounds of a Game, in every mode, come from eligible Entries that are Unknown or due for Review (ADR 0043), the rest from the pool. The answers of a 15-Round Game are 15 different Entries; «Виживання» and «На час», with no Round limit, reshuffle and ask them again once every eligible Entry has been asked. Eligibility is the Entries module's (ADR 0015): Hidden Entries, Hidden Categories and Retired Entries never appear as answers or Distractors. Playing writes no Seen, no History and no Active Day; a wrong answer marks the Entry Unknown, answers never move an interval, and the Round answers live until the result screen without being stored.

**Distractors** come from every eligible Entry of the Round's kind, not only from History, so four options always exist. A Word Round never offers an idiom, and an idiom Round never offers a Word.

- **«Вгадай слово»** gives a primary Definition (in its Rounds 6–15 a Word is asked by a gap in an Example or typed instead, ADR 0052). For a Word it picks Distractors by the Edition's Distractor rule (for `same-part-of-speech`, Words of the answer's part of speech first, then other eligible Words). It also asks about idioms of Idiom Type `idiom` (ADR 0046): the options are other eligible idioms drawn at random, shown as their expression with no Stress. No two options share a Lemma or expression, and no Distractor's primary Definition is the answer's (compared after case, runs of spaces and Stress marks are set aside). A place name or a proverb builds no Round and is never a Distractor (ADR 0046).
- **The Correction mode**'s Distractors are the main standard equivalents of other eligible Corrections, of the same Correction Type first, never one of the asked Correction's own equivalents. Corrections carry no authored distractors.

When a mode can make no fair Round of an Entry, such as a Word without three other Lemmas beside it, it skips that Entry.

**A Game Mode** is a value registered through a code-level Extension Point (ADR 0006); the Core's modes and an Edition's go through the same one. A mode names one kind of Entry, the one its card names and whose refusal it gives, and may name other kinds its Rounds also ask about («Вгадай слово» names Words and asks about idioms too). A further kind with no eligible Entry, such as Idioms out of the Mix, leaves the mode asking about the rest, so «Вгадай слово» never gives `idioms-out-of-mix` as its refusal.

**Round planning** alone decides whether a mode can start: the pool, History, the fair Rounds and, when they fall short of what the mode needs (15, or 8 Words with an origin for «Звідки слово»), the refusal. The offer and the start come from it, so a mode that can't start says why on its card before any tap. Why an Entry kind as a whole is not eligible, such as Corrections out of the Mix, is the Entries module's to say; the Game passes it on as the refusal. The Entries module reads the live Entries of the Language Pack once per app session and filters them by the eligible ids on each call. The composition opens without waiting for that read (about 0.4 s): the Game then plans its offers in the background, in turn with its other changes, and plans again after every hide, restore and Mix change. Until planned, an offer says so and its card can't start yet and gives no reason. A failed planning is tried again after 1 s, 2 s and 4 s through an injected `Schedule` port; when all fail, the offers stay unplanned until the composition's next `resume` (each return to the foreground and each new local day, ADR 0022) or an eligibility change.

**The Game** is a module of the Core composition (ADR 0018) with an immutable snapshot: the modes on offer, and the Game in play with its Round, Rounds passed, Lives and state. It lives in memory for the app session, not in the user state, so closing the app drops an unfinished Game. A 15-Round Game builds all its Rounds when it starts, and a mode with no Round limit builds more as it goes; a hide, a restore or a Mix change during a Game builds the Rounds still to come again, and the Rounds already shown stay.

**Each mode reaches its card in one shape.** A mode's offer carries the mode id, the Entry kind and, when it can't start, the refusal. The Game strings hold `game.modes`, one entry per mode id: the card's words, the label over its Rounds' prompt and a text per refusal it can give (every mode has one for too few eligible Entries; only a mode asking about Corrections has one for Corrections out of the Mix). An Edition's own mode brings its strings under its id in `game.modes` of its `strings.json`, checked by the same schema. The shell draws any offer as a card and names no mode. The app checks the modes' strings once the composition opens: a mode without them fails the launch, and one without a text for its refusal throws when its card is drawn.

**The Best result** is kept per Game Mode (ADR 0052): the most Rounds passed in one finished Game of the mode, or the right answers in «На час». Finished Games aren't kept, so each is its own user-state record, `game.best.<mode id>`, written only when a finished Game of the mode beats it, with a `highest` sync mark so sync keeps the higher of two devices' results.

## Considered Options

- **Seen Entries, as the master spec says**: rejected. The pool would collapse to the whole pack after every Cycle.
- **Distractors from History too**: rejected. A reader with 15 Words in History would get the same few Distractors in every Round.
- **Authored Distractors for each Correction**: rejected for now. Other Corrections' equivalents need no new content field.
- **Keep the Game in the user state**: rejected. An abandoned Game must never count, so a restart that drops it is simpler than migrating a half-played Game.
- **Build each Round when it comes, reading Words one by one**: rejected. The Distractor rule needs every eligible Word's part of speech.
- **A table of modes in the shell**: rejected. The shell decided which modes exist, and a mode it didn't know vanished.
- **Each mode value carries its own strings**: rejected. The Core holds no texts in any language.
- **Count eligible Entries for the offer, and let the start refuse**: rejected. A card would promise a Game the start refuses.
- **Plan the offers before the composition opens**: rejected. The launch would wait longer for a screen it doesn't show.
- **Retry forever, or show a reason once every try failed**: rejected. A pack that keeps failing to read is a broken install, and a reason would need a refusal text in every Edition for an error the reader can't fix.
- **A second clue when Definitions match**: rejected. A Round that needs a second clue is not a fair Round.
- **A mode that names a list of kinds, with no kind of its own**: rejected. «Вгадай слово» would refuse because Idioms are out of the Mix while its Words still play.
- **Rounds that ask for a place from its description**: rejected (ADR 0046).
