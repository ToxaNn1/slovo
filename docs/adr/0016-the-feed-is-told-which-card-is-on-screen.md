# The Feed is told which card is on screen and keeps the next card ready

The Core's Feed holds every rule that decides whether Seen is right. A shell tells it one thing: which card is on screen now, or that none is. The shell builds that from one signal, whether the Feed is in sight (Home focused, the app in the foreground, no onboarding over it), and reads the foreground from the one source it shares with the lifecycle adapter (ADR 0022). From that the Feed counts the dwell time of the card that leaves the screen, keeps the reader's place, and keeps the card after the one on screen ready, never more than one card ahead. Its cards are an array it never edits: each change makes a new one and tells the Feed's subscribers, so a shell subscribes (in React with `useSyncExternalStore`), turns its pager to the Feed's position after the cards change, and holds no Feed rules and no place of its own. The Feed opens in the Core composition with the user's words (ADR 0018).

**The next card.** The Feed's rule for the next card includes the Review slots of ADR 0043: a due Word in every 3rd Word slot and a due Correction in the Correction slot before a new one; a due Entry stays due until the reader marks it, and a Review overrides Seen.

**The reader's place.** The place is the card on screen, else the card the reader left, else the card the Feed opens on. A hide or a Mix change drops cards at once, the one on screen included, and an undo puts them back (ADR 0015). The Feed keeps the place in step, on screen or off it: the place stays on its card, goes to the next card when its card is dropped, and goes back to that card when its Entry is eligible again before the reader moved; leaving the screen and coming back to the same card is no move. Before any card was on screen, as under the onboarding's Mix step, the opening card's place goes on the same way, and when no card follows, the Feed gets the next one at once. A dropped card keeps its place among the session's cards, so an undo puts it back exactly where it left. Only a card on screen counts its dwell time; a card that leaves the screen through a drop is Seen by the dwell rule, and a card that takes the place while none is on screen starts its dwell, and the place is written, when the reader comes back.

**The Word of the Day.** The earliest Notification time of each local day carries that day's Word of the Day, so the notification plan (ADR 0021) and the Word of the Day agree on it. For today it is the Word the Word of the Day module chose, while it is unseen, and no other notification carries it. For a later day the plan Reserves a Word for that morning, and when the day comes the Word of the Day module takes the Word of the day's earliest planned notification, while it is still eligible, unseen and not the day before's Word. Otherwise the module keeps its rules: the next unseen Word in the Cycle order, never the day before's, chosen again when it is Hidden or Retired. When every Word of the Cycle is Seen but a Correction or an Idiom isn't, it takes a Seen Word, the next in the Cycle order after the day before's; the Cycle ends only when every eligible Entry in it is Seen, and only the Feed's end-of-Cycle card starts a new one.

The day can come before the Feed asks for its Word of the Day: the plan is made again on a new day, and a morning notification can open the app on its own Word. One module of the composition notices the new day (ADR 0022); it takes the morning Word before it plans and tells the Feed the day, so a passed morning notification is never dropped before its Word is taken, and a Word opened from it joins History as the Word of the Day's card. Taking the morning Word never chooses another Word and never starts a Cycle. The Feed opens a new day by the day it last opened with its Word of the Day, not by whether one is chosen. A Reserved Word may still come up in the Feed first: once it is Seen there, the plan gives its notification a new Word at once, and a hide, an unhide or a Mix change does the same for the Words no longer eligible.

## Considered Options

- **The shell pairs card-visible and card-hidden reports and asks for the next card itself**: rejected. Those rules decide whether Seen is right, yet they lived in an untested React hook, and the hook copied the card list after each call because the Feed never said when it changed.
- **A separate Core module over the Feed that turns screen reports into visible and hidden pairs**: rejected. It adds a second interface for one behaviour.
- **The shell keeps its pager index, or the card it last reported, and the Feed follows it**: rejected. A card dropped before the one on screen would move the reader on without a swipe, and the place rule would live in an untested hook that grows an input with each feature that covers the Feed.
- **Forget a dropped card and get it again like any next card**: rejected. Undo must put the card back where it was, not after the ready card.
- **Leaving the screen counts as a move**: rejected. The reader stayed on the card they left.
- **The plan reads the Word of the Day module for every day ahead, or the module keeps its own record of later days**: rejected. The module keeps only today's choice, choosing ahead would decide days the reader may never open, and a record of its own would repeat the plan records and could drift from them.
- **Choose the new day's Word of the Day only when the Feed asks**: rejected. A morning notification opened before the Feed, or a plan made at midnight, would leave the Feed to choose another Word, so the morning notification would not be the Word of the Day.
