# Synonym Row and Tip are the fourth and fifth kinds of Entry

Until 0.8.11 SLOVO had three kinds of Entry: Words, Corrections and Idioms (ADR 0046). Two kinds of knowledge a reader wants fitted none of them. A row of synonyms such as «іти → простувати, чимчикувати, шкандибати», each with its shade, is many words side by side, while a Word card shows one. A rule of good speech that is not one wrong form, such as «Котра година?» instead of «Скільки час?», the vocative «пане Андрію», how to sign a letter or where the comma goes, has nothing a Correction can swap. 0.8.11 (spec #730) adds the Synonym Row («синонімічний ряд»), the fourth kind of Entry, and the Tip («порада»), the fifth. Both are full Entries: they come in the Feed on turns of their own, sit in the Mix as wholes, take Knowledge Marks and come back for Review, work with Self-check, Favorites, Collections, History, hiding, Share Cards, notifications and the widget, and play in the Game. A Retired Synonym Row or Tip behaves like a Retired Word.

**Ids.** A Synonym Row id is `r_` followed by lowercase letters and digits; a Tip id is `t_` the same way. Both come from the Edition's append-only id registry (ADR 0010).

**The Synonym Row.** A dominant: its text with Stress and an optional Word id. A required short meaning line, which says the sense of the dominant the row is about. 5 to 12 members, each its text with Stress, an optional Word id and an optional shade note of at most 60 characters («повільно», «кульгаючи»); a member without a note shows plainly. An optional example sentence, and at least one source. No member goes without Stress, and a row has no Category, rarity or Pronunciation. The dominant and each member carry their Stress as a Lemma does, the positions of their stressed vowels, never as marks in their text. The Edition authors rows in `content/synonym-rows`, the Language Pack build writes them into tables of their own and records `synonymRow` as a kind of Entry, and the Core's content read port reads them back, a link to a Retired Word left out. Its card shows the chip «синоніми», and a long row wraps and scrolls inside its card. A member that links a Word opens that Word on a tap, even a Hidden Word or one of a Category out of the Mix, because an explicit tap always opens its Entry (ADR 0015); a member without a link is plain text.

**The back-link.** Nothing new is authored on a Word. The Core derives, for every Word, the Synonym Rows that link it as a member, and the Word card model carries the first such row's dominant and id, which the Word card shows as «У синонімічному ряду: {домінанта}» and opens on a tap. A Word in no row shows no such line.

**The Tip.** Required: a title, the rule in a few words («Котра година?»); the standard form; an explanation of at most 300 characters; at least one source; and a Game question, a prompt with one right answer and three wrong ones, all different. Optional: the non-standard form and an example. A Tip has no Category, rarity or Pronunciation. The Game question holds exactly three wrong answers. The Edition authors Tips in `content/tips`, the Language Pack build writes them into tables of their own and records `tip` as a kind of Entry, and the Core's content read port reads them back. Its card shows the chip «порада» and reuses the Correction card's labels: the non-standard form, when there is one, under «Так часто кажуть», the standard form under «Українською краще». A Tip with no non-standard form, such as the vocative in an address, shows only the right way and invents no wrong form.

**Tip or Correction.** One form swapped for one standard form is a Correction; a rule, a construction, etiquette or punctuation is a Tip. A Tip may show a non-standard form so it reads like a Correction where it can, but what it teaches is the rule, not the swap.

**The build refuses**, with the reason: an unknown Word id on a dominant or a member; a row with fewer than 5 or more than 12 members; two members with the same text, in lower case; Stress on a letter of a row's text that is not a vowel, or past its end; a shade note over 60 characters; a Tip explanation over 300 characters; a Tip without a Game question, with other than one right and three wrong answers, or with two answers the same, in lower case; either kind without a source. The Language Pack build is the one place these rules live, and the content CLI (`pnpm content new synonym-row|tip`, `edit`, `retire`, `check`) calls the same rules. A Word that is Retired later loses its links silently: the member or the dominant stays as plain text, as a Correction's link to a Retired Word does.

**Pack format 5.** An app on pack format 4 can't read the new kinds, so the build writes pack format 5 and the app reads only it, through the one constant `PACK_FORMAT` (ADR 0032). Packs of format 4 are no longer published, since there are no readers before 1.0.

**The Feed turns.** A Synonym Row's turn is every 9th card of the Cycle while the Cycle has unseen rows, and a Tip's every 11th card while it has unseen Tips, each counted from its kind's last card, like the Correction's turn on every 5th card and the Idiom's on every 7th. When turns fall on one card the order is Correction, Idiom, Synonym Row, Tip, and each loser takes the next card. A row or a Tip due for Review takes its kind's next turn before a new one, as Corrections and Idioms do (ADR 0043). A row or a Tip is Seen after the same dwell time as any Entry. The Word of the Day and the Review slot stay Words only.

**The Mix and eligibility.** The Mix gains two wholes, Synonym Rows and Tips, like Corrections and Idioms (ADR 0019). An Eligible Entry covers them the same way: not Retired, its kind on in the Edition and not off remotely (ADR 0040), its kind in the Mix and not Hidden. The Feature Switches `synonymRows` and `tips` are new Edition fields, required of every Edition and on in the Ukrainian Edition, and so are the two fields of the Edition's default Mix that say whether a new user has each kind in, both in for the Ukrainian Edition; an Edition with a kind off shows no Mix switch, no Hidden section and no turns for it. The Mix screen and the Hidden screen name the two «Синонімічні ряди» and «Поради», with the proposed Mix subtitles «Різні слова для одного значення» and «Як говорити й писати по-українському». The choice is user state the Core owns (ADR 0013) and syncs last write wins like the rest of the Mix (ADR 0041). Sync already carries any Entry id, so Favorites, Collections, History, Seen, Knowledge Marks, Review and Hidden Entries need no new record kind.

**Self-check.** A row hides everything but its dominant («іти → ?»); a Tip hides everything but its title and its non-standard form, or only its title when it has none. The Knowledge Mark buttons work after the reveal, as on every card.

**Share Card, notifications and the widget.** The Core decides every text. A row's Share Card shows the dominant, the meaning and the first 6 members, then «…» when there are more; a Tip's shows the title, the non-standard form and the standard form. A row's notification and widget text is the dominant with its first 3 members («іти → простувати, чимчикувати, шкандибати…»); a Tip's is its title with «Так: …» and the standard form. The Widget Snapshot gains the two kinds, with the same headline, second line and VoiceOver text as every Entry (ADR 0026). The widget never reads `kind`, so it shows them as they are: the Widget Snapshot format stays 1, and there is no native change. A tap on the widget or a notification opens the row or Tip like any Entry.

**The Game.** The Core Game Mode draws Synonym Rows and Tips among its eligible Entries like Idioms, with no quota. A row's Round asks «Що є синонімом до „{домінанта}“?»: the right answer is one of its members, and the three Distractors are members of other rows whose text is not in the asked row, so no Round has two right answers. Rows play only while the Mix holds at least 4 rows. A Tip's Round is its own Game question. A mistake marks the Entry Unknown, and Game answers never move a Review (ADR 0043). In the Game Review the first tap expands a row's members or a Tip's explanation, and the second opens the Entry. The Core decides each item of the Game Review (`buildGameReview`): what it leads with (a row's dominant with its Stress, a Tip's title), the reader's wrong pick, what it expands into and the Entry the second tap opens. What rows and Tips do in the escalating Rounds is decided in 0.8.12.

## Considered Options

- **Synonym Rows as Synonyms on each Word**: rejected. A Word's Synonyms help its own meaning; a row's members are mostly not Words of the pack, and their shades belong to the row, not to any one Word.
- **Authoring the back-link on the Word**: rejected. The same fact would live in two places and drift; the Core derives it from the rows.
- **Tips as Corrections, or a new Correction Type**: rejected. Many Tips have no non-standard form, and a Correction's card, Game Round and Correction mode all need the pair.
- **Tips as Idioms**: rejected. A Tip teaches a rule with a right form, not the meaning of a fixed expression.
- **One kind for both**: rejected. A row and a Tip share no field beyond the sources, so every screen would branch on a flag.
- **Categories and rarity on rows and Tips**: rejected. The Mix takes each kind in or out as a whole, as it does Idioms, and the Rarity Floor is a measure of Words.
- **Keep pack format 4 and let an older app skip what it can't read**: rejected (ADR 0032).
- **A new Widget Snapshot format**: rejected. The format changes only for what the widget can't read, and the widget draws the new kinds from the text it already reads.
- **Synonym Rows or Tips as the Word of the Day or in the Review slot**: rejected. The morning stays a Word.
