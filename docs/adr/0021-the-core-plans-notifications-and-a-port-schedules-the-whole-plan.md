# The Core plans notifications, and a port schedules the whole plan

Each notification carries one Word the reader hasn't Seen. Which Word comes at which time is a rule about eligibility, Seen and the Cycle, so the Core decides it, in the Notifications module of the composition (ADR 0018). The Core declares a notification scheduler port and the shell implements it on `expo-notifications`. The port reports the permission (allowed, denied or not asked yet), asks for it, and replaces every pending notification with a given plan. Tests use a fake that keeps the last plan and lets the test set the permission.

**The plan** is a list of notifications, each a local date, a local time and one Word, with its text and sound. The title is the Lemma with its Stress marks; the body is the primary Definition, cut at a word boundary to 80 characters with the Edition's ellipsis. The sound is the Edition's own file (`{ file }`, ADR 0023) when it has one and the reader's sound switch is on, the system sound when the Edition has none, and none when the switch is off, so the scheduler adapter plays exactly what the plan says.

- **Floating local time.** The plan holds local dates and times, never instants, and the port schedules each at that wall-clock time in the device's current time zone. 09:00 stays 09:00 after travel and across daylight-saving changes.
- **The 14-day and 64 bounds.** The plan starts from the next Notification time still ahead today (a time due this minute has passed) and holds 14 days of times from there, never more than 64, the most iOS keeps pending for one app.
- **Reserved as plan records.** Each notification is one user-state record, `notifications.plan.` + local date + `T` + local time, holding the Word's id (ADR 0013). A Word with such a record is Reserved; Reserved is never stored on its own. No Word is Reserved twice, and only Words are.
- **Choosing Words.** Only eligible Words unseen in the current Cycle. Planning again keeps every notification still ahead whose Word is still eligible, unseen and not needed elsewhere (today's Word of the Day goes only to this morning's notification), and drops those that passed or whose times were removed. A new or emptied notification takes the next unseen Word in the Cycle order after the furthest Word planned before, and the unseen Words before it only once those run out. With fewer unseen Words than notifications, the latest stay empty: only the Feed starts a new Cycle.
- **The morning notification is the Word of the Day.** The earliest time of each local day carries that day's Word of the Day, and the Word of the Day module takes a later day's Word from its morning notification.
- **The Feed doesn't skip Reserved Words** (owner). Once a Reserved Word is Seen in the Feed, the plan is made again at once and its notification gets a new Word.
- **Whole-plan replacement.** Every plan replaces everything scheduled before, so the device never drifts from the Core's plan. The plan is made when the composition opens, on the return to the foreground and on a new local day, when a time is added, changed or removed, when the sound is switched, when the reader allows notifications, when eligibility changes and when the Feed makes a Reserved Word Seen.
- **Permission.** While the device doesn't allow notifications the plan is empty and nothing is Reserved; the Notification times stay stored, so notifications start at once when allowed. The onboarding's «Дозволити сповіщення» and, while permission is not asked yet, the Notifications screen ask through the same call; iOS lists an app in its notification settings only once it has asked, so «Пізніше» must not close the door. Once the reader has answered, asking again asks nothing and the screen opens the iOS settings. The Settings row reads «Вимкнено» while permission is not allowed.
- **Feature Switches.** The plan reads the effective values of two Feature Switches (ADR 0040) each time it plans. With **notifications** off the plan is empty and nothing is Reserved, as without permission. With **the Word of the Day** off the earliest time of a day is a notification like the others.

**The onboarding's sample** is the next morning notification of a plan made now as if the device let the app notify: this morning's while its time is ahead, else the next day's. Asking for it writes no plan and Reserves nothing; like a plan, it first lets the day change take the morning Word of a day the composition hasn't turned to (ADR 0022). With the notifications switch off there is no next morning. With no Notification time left there is no morning notification, and the sample is today's Word of the Day, unseen, with no time. The Core onboarding takes the sample from Notifications; the shell only draws it.

The plan records and the times stay on the device under sync (ADR 0013's sync marks), since each device plans from its own permission and local time.

## Considered Options

- **Instants in the plan**: rejected. A flight or a daylight-saving change would move 09:00 until the app is opened again, and the plan may run two weeks without an open.
- **Reserved as its own record per Word**: rejected. It would have to be kept in step with the plan records, and a crash between the two would leave a Word Reserved by no notification.
- **A port that adds and cancels single notifications**: rejected. The Core would have to know what the device still holds, and a missed cancel would leave a stale Word on the device.
- **The Feed skips Reserved Words**: rejected by the owner. A reader who opens the app often would see the Feed jump over the Words waiting for their notifications.
- **Refill new notifications from the start of the Cycle order**: rejected. A notification that passed unopened frees the earliest unseen Word, so the newest notification would bring it straight back.
- **A plan as far as 64 notifications reach**: rejected. With one time a day it would reserve two months of Words, which hiding, the Mix and the Feed would keep invalidating.
- **Plan for real before the reader is asked, so the sample reads the stored plan**: rejected. Words would be Reserved for notifications that may never be allowed.
- **The shell builds the sample**: rejected. It would repeat the plan's rule for a passed morning and a Seen Word of the Day.
- **Ask for permission only from the onboarding**: rejected. A reader who chose «Пізніше» would never get notifications.
