# A switched-off feature is absent

Owner decision of 2026-11-17, after the app pass. This ADR supersedes ADR 0073 (the traced Daily Crossword), amends ADR 0087 (the crossing crossword is no longer behind its own switch), ADR 0052 and ADR 0089 (what a Feature Switch's `off` means), and amends ADR 0122 for one more change an admin output may make.

**What went wrong.** Several Feature Switches did not hide their feature when off: they brought back an older version of it. With `crossingCrossword` off the app showed the traced 5×5 grid of ADR 0073; with `tracedCrossword` off as well, the typed crossword of ADR 0052. Every such switch kept two or three versions of one feature alive in the code, the tests, the strings and the admin.

**The rule.** A Feature Switch turns one feature on or off; off, the feature is absent from the app. It never chooses between an old and a new version. When a new version of a feature replaces the old one, the old code, its tests, strings, tokens and Feature Switch go in the same change; the feature keeps the one switch that makes it absent.

**The Daily Crossword.** `tracedCrossword`, `crossingCrossword` and `dailyCrosswordShare` are deleted. `dailyCrossword` on is the crossing crossword on the Letter Wheel of ADR 0087; off, there is no Daily Crossword card and its screen goes back to the Game. Its parts `crosswordHint`, `crosswordSolveTime`, `crosswordSolvedScreen` and `crosswordBadgeStamp` (with `badges`) now depend on `dailyCrossword`. The traced grid, its screen, card, Share Card, strings and tokens are gone; a solved crossword is shared from «Розгадано!» as ADR 0087 states. The user state record `crossword.traced` stays reserved (ADR 0013): nothing reads or writes it.

**The API.** A retired Feature Switch leaves the `states` of every admin answer. The admin ships with the backend and its client validates no answer (ADR 0170), so the breaking-change check skips a field gone from `body.states` on a `/v1/admin/` path; any other admin output field that goes still breaks `/v1` (amends ADR 0122).

**Retired by the same rule.** `gameThreeLives` (a Game has 3 Lives), `timedTwentySeconds` («На час» lasts 20 seconds and ends on «Фініш») and `newLevelScreen` (a new Level shows the new Level screen). `widgetHint` is deleted with its feature (owner decision of 2026-11-18): no sheet follows a Game, its «Фініш» poster stays clean, and Settings › «Віджети» alone opens the widget guide; the record `game.widgetHintOffered` stays reserved (ADR 0013). With `dailyCrossword` off, its Badges are absent too. Off in the UA Edition, the owner kept the old version of three (2026-11-18): `letterTiles` («За літерами» is typed), `survivalStreak` («Виживання» counts the Rounds passed) and `survivalOneLife` («Виживання» keeps the Game's 3 Lives); `pairsStay` («Пари» wait for «Далі» after each pair), `buildIdiomCheck` («Склади вислів» has only the idiom's words and takes the answer on the last chip) and `livesOverWarmup` (a Game out of Lives ends on «ФІНІШ», no «Розминка!»). On in the UA Edition, their new version kept: `quietCategories` (a quiet Category always draws quiet), `collectionBoardStyle` (the Collections and a Collection's screen follow board 09 alone), `gameAnswerCard` (the sheet after an answer is the answer card, in the Game and in the Feed) `categoryKnownFirst` (a Category's screen lists its Known Entries first), `finishPoster` (every Game ends on the «Фініш» poster), `gameAnswerBanner` (the answer banner always follows an answer) and `gameQuestionOnGround` (the Round's question stands on the ground, with no card) and `gameOptionStamp` (a Game Round's options take the board's look: no key, a short one large, the ✓ stamp, the wrong text crossed out) and `noCounters` (Settings always has «Без лічильників», which hides every counter; «Приховати Серію» is gone) and `badgeToast` (a Badge earned toasts in a Game and is stamped elsewhere; the Mascot alone and its Badge line are gone) and `eveningNotification` (a day brings at most 2 notifications: a Word at its earliest Notification time and the evening notification at its latest; the times between bring none) and `swipeMarks` (a side swipe or the «⋯» sheet sets a Knowledge Mark; heart, Collections and «⋯» stand in the card's corner; the action row, its «Знаю»/«Не знаю» buttons, its Community Counters line and the kept hint under it are gone) and `savedSwipesOff` (an Entry opened from a tab of «Збережене» takes no side swipes, its Knowledge Mark set from the «⋯» sheet, ADR 0144) and `textSizeSteps` (the text size is the three-step ladder and its screen «Шрифт і розмір»; the S…XL scale and its row are gone, and the record `settings.fontSize` stays reserved, ADR 0013) and `rarityRange` (with `rarityCeiling` on, «Рідкість» is the picker of both ends of the Rarity Range and every surface obeys both; the «до 1»…«до 5» row is gone) and `dashboard` (the Dashboard is always the home; its Word of the Day hero shows while `wordOfTheDay` is on, every other block keeps its own switch, and no switch needs `dashboard` any more) and `offlinePlate` (no connection is said on the offline plate alone; the old toast, the Account's line and «Синхронізація чекає на мережу» are gone) and `plainGameReview` (the Game Review is the plain list «Огляд раундів», ✓ or ✗ and the word; a row neither expands nor opens the Entry, and the Game tab has no Entry screen of its own) and `gameHubGroups` (the Games hub always stands in the five groups «Звук», «Значення», «Букви», «Швидкість», «Нове», its cards coming in a cascade) and `gameHubRecord` (a Games hub row always carries its record, «Рекорд: 12 з 15» or «Ще жодної гри») and `wordTabs` (every Entry card of the Feed, a Category's Feed and Entry details is the tabbed card in its fixed frame; the Feed's header has no XP bar) and `entryPanelAllKinds` (Entry details head every kind as a Word; no Entry card under a bare panel).

**Still to do.** None: every switch whose `off` showed an older version is retired.
