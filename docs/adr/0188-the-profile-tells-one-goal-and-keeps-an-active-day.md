# The Profile tells one goal and keeps an Active Day

Owner's app pass of 2026-11-18 (items 46–54). This ADR amends ADR 0074 (the Active Day under a goal set later that day), ADR 0066 (a Badge's progress, the counters) and ADR 0145 (the Level card).

**An Active Day stays one.** A day that reached 5 XP while no Daily Goal was in force stays an Active Day when a goal is set later that day, as a day met stays met. Before, setting a goal took the day back: a Streak Badge earned with today counted («Три дні поспіль») stayed earned while the Streak and the Best Streak fell below it. XP earned once the goal is in force makes no Active Day; the day is active once the goal is met. A day whose goal is turned off («Без цілі») counts by 5 XP, as before.

**Today waiting for its goal.** While today has a Daily Goal not met and is not yet an Active Day, the Profile says so under the Active Days calendar: today counts once the goal is met, with today's Words Seen of the goal.

**One goal on the Level card.** The card measures to one mark. From Level 1 it is the next name, as the board: the ring and «{xp} / {mark} XP» in total XP, then «{name}: ще N XP». Under the last name it is the next Level. Before Level 1 there is no Level to show: the ring holds the XP with «XP» under it, the lines read «{xp} / {first} XP» and «ще N XP до рівня 1», and the name header shows the Level's name alone, never «Рівень 0».

**The Level card's colours.** The card takes the tile surface and the ink text of the theme, not the inverted ink card of the board: it no longer stands as a light patch on the dark page.

**A Badge's progress.** A Badge not yet earned shows the progress of its best reach, the one that earns it: «Тиждень поспіль» after a best run of 2 days reads 2 of 7, not the 0 of today's Streak.

**The counters.** With «Без лічильників» on, the Badges keep their marks, but their counts («1 з 21», a family's «0 з 4») are hidden.
