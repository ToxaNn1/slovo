# «SLOVO пропонує» is the Curated Collections rail above the Categories

Owner decision of spec #1992, ticket #1994. This ADR amends ADR 0076, 0081, 0082 and 0105.

**Why.** Curated Collections («Добірка», ADR 0076) were off in the UA Edition (ADR 0105), and their «Добірки» rail looked unlike every other rail of the Dashboard. The owner wants them back as editorial picks that read as part of the Categories.

**The rail.** With `curatedCollections` on, the Dashboard shows the rail «SLOVO пропонує» (Edition string `dashboard.curatedCollectionsTitle`) right after «Попередні слова» and right before «Категорії»; off, it is absent (ADR 0176). It is drawn from the Categories rail's own header and tile components, so it looks and behaves like them: the header counts every Curated Collection published by today in the local calendar; the rail keeps at most ten cards, the unopened ones first, then the newest. A tile shows a pictogram, the collection's name and its count of Entries, the small mark «Нова» until it is opened and the Edition's added mark (UA «У Міксі ✓») once it is copied; a tap opens the collection's screen of ADR 0082. The header opens the grid of every Curated Collection published by today, in the rail's order, drawn from the Categories' grid k1 (ADR 0081) under the Edition string `dashboard.curatedCollectionsGridTitle` (UA «Добірки»); its tile is the rail's and opens the same screen, and opening that screen from either door clears «Нова». A Curated Collection may hold Entries of any kind. With `curatedCollectionFeed` on (it needs `curatedCollections`; on in UA), a tap on an Entry of a collection's screen opens the collection's Feed, the Category's Feed of ADR 0110 over the collection's live Entries in its order, from the tapped one and round again endlessly, each kind on its own card with the kicker `dashboard.curatedCollection.feedKicker` (UA «Добірка · {name} · {place} з {of}»), making nothing Seen and leaving History as it was; off, the tap opens the Entry's card as before.

**The pictogram.** A Curated Collection carries an optional `pictogram`: the id of a Category or Correction Category whose pictogram its tile draws. Unset, the tile draws the Edition's `curatedCollectionPictogram` (UA `language-speech`), itself the id of an existing Category; with neither, no pictogram. The Language Pack build refuses a `pictogram` that names no Category, and the Edition config refuses such a default; the pack format is 14.

**The publish date.** A Curated Collection's `publishedOn` is optional; empty means today. The backend keeps `appearedAt`, the moment the collection appeared, as its own column, never in a Patch: a save with `publishedOn` today or earlier sets it to the save's moment, a later date sets it to 00:00 Europe/Kyiv of that day, and an edit that leaves the date unchanged keeps it. Import and migration 0033 set it to the start of `publishedOn`. Once `appearedAt` has passed, a change of `publishedOn` is refused by the content rule `curated-collection-appeared`; the admin shows the date read-only. The app never shows the date. Added by ticket #1997.

**Retiring.** A Curated Collection can be retired and brought back like any other record (`retired`, default false): the admin «Добірки» editor has «Вивести з ужитку» and «Повернути в ужиток» and marks it «виведено», and the content queue's rollback restores it. A Retired collection leaves circulation: the Language Pack holds no rows of it, as with a Retired Crossword day, so a Patch removes it from the reader's pack (format stays 14); the Core leaves it out of the rail, its count, the grid, its screen, its Feed and «Додати собі». A reader who already added it keeps their own copy in the Mix. Its `appearedAt` stays, so a bring-back keeps the publish date. Added by ticket #2022.

**Amends.** ADR 0076 and 0081: the rail is named «SLOVO пропонує» and comes before «Категорії», not after. ADR 0082: the Curated Collection screen stays, its entry point is the new tile. ADR 0105: the UA Edition turns `curatedCollections` on. Under ADR 0176 the new look replaces the old «Добірки» rail code in the same change; no switch keeps the old rail.

**Consequences.** One more rail on the UA Dashboard; editors pick a pictogram per collection or rely on the default.
