## Shortfall

`source:corrections` reads only goroh «Слововживання», and a draft is ready only when the article prints an example pair. That pair comes almost only from OnlineCorrector articles; «Мова – не калька», «Словник-антисуржик» and goroh's own notes give the standard form without one. Of 592 forms tried: 344 had no article (no-entry), 189 an article without an example pair or about another form (missing-data), 52 a ready draft, of which 27 are kept; the rest named the wrong form (an article about -уч- participles under a paronym, a paronym pair read the wrong way round, a spelling article) or shared a root with the live content.

- **anglicism**: goroh has almost no articles on English borrowings (лайфхак, фідбек, хайп, кешбек… no-entry); the ones it has come from «Мова – не калька» without an example pair and with purist equivalents (гаджет → знадіб, шоу → розважайлівка) that the Edition would not ship.
- **surzhyk**: articles exist for most everyday forms, but none prints an example pair.
- **wrongStress**: goroh has no stress articles for 45 more forms; the articles it shows are about the word's idioms.
- **paronym**: OnlineCorrector covers few pairs beyond the 16 already live.
