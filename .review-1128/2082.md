# Review of #2082: Statistics consent in the EEA (ADR 0210)

Diff: `origin/main...origin/feat/2082-eea-statistics-consent`. One finding, low severity. No send path skips the consent.

## Findings (most severe first)

### 1. The welcome line can say statistics are on when the Privacy row is hidden: CONFIRMED (low)

- **Where:** `packages/core/src/onboarding/onboarding.ts:234` (`statisticsLine`); compare `apps/mobile/src/shown.ts:114`.
- **Defect:** `statisticsLine` checks only `statisticsConsent`, the region and the reader's choice. It ignores whether anything that switch governs is on (`analytics`, `communityCounters`). «Конфіденційність» shows the switch only while `communityCounters || analytics` is on.
- **Failure scenario:** A non-EEA reader, with a Rule or an Edition that turns off both `analytics` and `communityCounters`, and `statisticsConsent` on. The welcome says «Анонімна статистика ввімкнена. Вимкнути: Налаштування → Конфіденційність.», but no Analytics event or Community Counter can leave the device and the row the line points to is not there. A probe on the branch's own harness (`run({ region: 'UA', on: [knowledge, wordOfTheDay, questionnaire, statisticsConsent] })`) gave `statisticsLine: true` with no events and no counters sent. Questionnaire answer counters still go out in that state, so the reader would be told to turn them off in a row that does not exist.
- **Fix:** Also require `edition.isFeatureEnabled('analytics') || edition.isFeatureEnabled('communityCounters')`, the same condition as the Privacy row, so the line and the row share one source. The same guard could also skip the consent step when that row is hidden.

## Checked, no defect

1. **Nothing before the choice.** Every sender goes through `statistics-consent.ts`:
   - Analytics `track` uses `sharedBy`. That covers composition, Settings, Account (via `...ports`), Questionnaire, Game, Search and Notifications.
   - Counter deltas, counter sending and the onboarding's questionnaire counters use `sharesStatistics`.
   - Mobile passes `consent` to the composition, `openCounterSending` and `openDeviceAccount` through `DevicePorts`, and to `settingsOpening`.
   - `port.track` has no other caller. The PostHog client is created lazily with `captureAppLifecycleEvents: false`.
   - An undefined region counts as the EEA.
2. **Asked once.**
   - `statisticsConsentPending` asks only while the record is `undefined`. A choice from the step or from «Конфіденційність» is never asked again.
   - An updating install that finished the onboarding keeps the switch off and is not asked (`finished` state), as ADR 0210 says.
   - A queue of counters left from before the update stays unsent until the reader allows.
3. **Welcome line.** The line is false for an EEA or unknown-region reader, for a reader who turned statistics off, and while `statisticsConsent` is off. Finding 1 is the one exception.
4. **Skip.** `finish()` on the consent step writes no `shareCounters`, so the switch stays off. `countSkipped` sends nothing for this step. `next()` without a choice does the same, and the UI offers no «Далі». A «Не дозволяти» sends no switch-off unit.
5. **Code rules.** Core has no React import. The diff adds no `as`, `!` or `@ts-expect-error`. The "sends nothing" tests are paired with a non-EEA test that proves the harness sends, so they would fail on a no-op.
