# Audit 1121, group a1

Scope: `packages/core` games (`*-mode.ts`, `guess-word.ts`, `*-form.ts`, `crossing-crossword.ts`, `game-mode.ts`, `fakes/*`), the tests of the Feed and Progress zones, and a review of PR #2088 (ADR 0205, the XP day fold) at `272d012c9`. Read-only; scratch tests were run and deleted.

## PR #2088: answers to the three questions

- **Can two devices double XP?** No, as far as traced. Every reader goes through `readXpLog`, which keys by record id; an XP day and a single record of one id count once (`xp-days.ts:97-113`), the derivations of `xp-log.ts` place records by instant and id, not by the order read, and two devices that fold the same records make the same key.
- **Can XP be lost?** On the device, no. On the Account, yes, for a while or for good: a1.1.
- **Can the tombstone cap lose a deletion that matters?** Yes, and sooner than ADR 0205 says: a1.2.
- **Does the fold break Badges, the Streak, the Data export?** Not in the Core (same records, Active Days are their own records, the export reads through `readXpLog`). It does break the admin Account page (a1.3), and one bad XP day stops the app opening (a1.4).

## a1.1 The push deletes the folded records from the Account before their XP day is there

- **Status:** CONFIRMED (reproduced by a scratch test). **Severity:** medium
- **Where:** `packages/core/src/sync/exchange.ts:327-347`; `packages/core/src/progress/xp-days.ts:17-20` (2000 records a launch)
- **Defect:** "Deletions first" orders the whole queue, and each batch of 500 is its own push. A fold of more than about 500 records (the first fold of any long log: up to 2000) sends its tombstones in batches 1-4 and the XP days that hold those records only in the last batch. If a push between them fails (offline, app killed, `TOO_MANY_REQUESTS`, a batch refused for good), the Account holds the tombstones and not the XP days.
- **Consequence:** Reproduced: 600 old Rounds, the first deletion batch accepted, the next push failing: a device signed in anew reads 100 XP of 600. It lasts until the folding device syncs again; if that device is lost, reinstalled or signed out first, the XP, the Badges' sources and the Streak's freezes of those days are gone from the Account for good. Before the PR those records were safe on the Account.
- **Fix:** Send each XP day in the same push as the deletions of its own rows (a kept deletion already frees room for the other changes of its push), or send the XP days first when the Account has room; never a tombstone of a record before the XP day that holds it is accepted.

## a1.2 The 20 000 tombstones are filled by tombstones no device needs, so a deletion that matters is lost after months, not years

- **Status:** PLAUSIBLE (row creation traced; the rate is arithmetic). **Severity:** medium
- **Where:** `apps/backend/src/sync/user-records.ts:78-116`; `packages/core/src/progress/xp-days.ts:119-122,157`; `packages/core/src/user-state/tracked-user-state.ts:83-91`
- **Defect:** Every folded `xp.` record leaves a tombstone row with a fresh sequence, also for a key the Account never held (the insert writes a row for every pushed tombstone). Devices ignore `xp.` tombstones (`exchange.ts:254`), yet they are the bulk of the cap: an active reader writes 30-100 XP records a day, so 20 000 tombstones pass in about 7 to 22 months, not "years of ordinary use" (ADR 0205). Worse, a guest tracks pending changes too: years of folds leave thousands of pending `xp.` tombstones that all reach the Account at the first sign-in and push out that many real tombstones at once. Nothing tells a device that its cursor is older than a deleted tombstone.
- **Consequence:** A device away for that long (or signed out and in again, which re-sends every record at `FIRST_SIGN_IN`) keeps a Word removed from Saved, a Knowledge Mark taken back or a deleted Collection, and at re-sign-in brings it back to every device, since the row that would refuse the stale change is gone.
- **Fix:** Keep `xp.` tombstones out of the cap without reading keys: write no row for a tombstone of a key that holds no record, and let the client drop the pending tombstone of a `union` record whose XP day is pending in the same queue only after both are sent. Keep the lowest deleted sequence per Account and Edition and answer a pull from before it with "start anew", so a late device re-reads the whole state instead of missing deletions in silence.

## a1.3 The admin Account page reads only `xp.` records, so folded days vanish from it

- **Status:** CONFIRMED (traced). **Severity:** medium
- **Where:** `apps/admin/src/account-records.ts:320-328,354,362`
- **Defect:** The page builds XP, the Daily Goal, freezes, Breaks and Badges from keys under `xp.` alone. After the fold those keys are tombstones (not parsed, counted as "other") and the records live under `xpDay.`, a prefix the page does not know.
- **Consequence:** For every Account that has folded, the admin shows the XP, Level, Badges, freezes and Breaks of the last 30 days only, and a reset marker older than 30 days is not seen, so the numbers are wrong both ways. Support decisions are made on it.
- **Fix:** Read the log in the admin the way the Core does: unfold `xpDay.` rows by id beside the `xp.` records (share `readXpLog`'s row reading through the contracts or the Core's public seam).

## a1.4 One XP day with an out-of-range `t` stops the app opening, on every device, for good

- **Status:** CONFIRMED (reproduced by a scratch test). **Severity:** low
- **Where:** `packages/core/src/progress/xp-days.ts:70-71`; `packages/core/src/progress/records.ts:133-136`; `packages/core/src/progress/xp-log.ts:335`
- **Defect:** `XpDaySchema` takes any JSON as a row, and `recordOf` calls `new Date(base + t).toISOString()` on any number. A `t` such as `1e20` throws `RangeError: Invalid time value` out of `readXpLog`, so out of `openXpLog` and `openComposition`. Outside data is not parsed where it enters.
- **Consequence:** Reproduced: `openComposition` rejects. The record comes by Sync to every device of the Account, and it cannot be removed by Sync, since a `union` record ignores tombstones. It needs a broken or tampered device, hence low.
- **Fix:** Validate a row in the schema (`t` a safe integer within a few days of the day) or guard `recordOf` so a row that is no record is skipped, as the comment above it already promises.

# Games and fakes

## a1.5 «Вгадай слово» can offer a second right answer (a synonym, or a Word whose extra Definition is the prompt)

- Status: CONFIRMED (reproduced)
- Severity: medium
- Where: `packages/core/src/game/guess-word.ts:270-277` (Word Round), `:283-289` (idiom Round); `distractorOrder` at `:28-37`; the same rule in `packages/core/src/game/pairs-mode.ts:57-67`
- Defect: Distractors are left out only when their *primary* Definition has the same key as the answer's. A Word whose Lemma is in `answer.synonyms`, or whose `extraDefinitions` hold the prompt, can still be a Distractor. `distractorOrder` puts same-part-of-speech Words first, and synonyms are of the same part of speech by ADR 0146, so these Words are the most likely to be picked. Repro: answer «баский» (synonyms `['показний']`, primary «Гарний, показний.») and «показний» (extra Definition «Гарний, показний.»). The Round offered `[баский, сад, кіт, показний]`.
- Consequence: a Round has two options that fit the prompt. A reader who picks the synonym is judged wrong, loses a Life, and the Word gets an Unknown mark. A pairs grid can be ambiguous the same way.
- Fix: in `buildRound` for Words, also leave out candidates whose Lemma key is in `answer.synonyms`, and candidates for which any Definition of either Word (`primaryDefinition` and `extraDefinitions`) matches the prompt's key. Apply the same check to idiom Rounds and to `pairsMode`'s grid.

## a1.6 «Впиши слово» judges a listed standard equivalent wrong

- Status: CONFIRMED (reproduced)
- Severity: low (ADR 0146 asks for one equivalent per Correction; the schema allows more)
- Where: `packages/core/src/game/type-word-mode.ts:22,27`; `packages/core/src/game/typed-form.ts:71-80`; schema `packages/contracts/src/correction.ts:131-135`
- Defect: `expected` is only `standardEquivalents[0]`, and `typedForm.judge` compares against that one text. `correctionMode` treats every equivalent as right: it leaves all of them out of the Distractors (`correction-mode.ts:39`). The typed mode does not do the same.
- Consequence: with the test fixture `correction()` (`['брати', 'also брати']`), typing the second equivalent gives `right: false`. Any Correction with two equivalents takes a Life for a correct answer.
- Fix: give `TypedContent` an `accepted: readonly string[]` (defaulting to `[expected]`) and judge `right` as any accepted text matching. Or, if one equivalent is the rule, enforce it in the schema with `.max(1)` for the types that play.

## a1.7 A gap Example still shows the Lemma when the Lemma appears twice

- Status: CONFIRMED (reproduced)
- Severity: low
- Where: `packages/core/src/game/guess-word.ts:108-125` (`gapIn`)
- Defect: only the first whole-word match is replaced with `…`. Repro: «Кіт ловить мишей, бо кіт голодний.» became «… ловить мишей, бо кіт голодний.».
- Consequence: Rounds 6–10 give the answer away in the prompt.
- Fix: skip an Example that holds the Lemma more than once (search again from `found.end`), or replace every occurrence. Replacing every occurrence needs a highlight per gap, so skipping is simpler.

## a1.8 Fake backend serves Sync without any Session

- Status: CONFIRMED (reproduced)
- Severity: medium (test fidelity)
- Where: `packages/core/src/fakes/fake-backend.ts:374-407` (`sync.push` / `sync.pull` never call `sessionOf`)
- Defect: the contract makes Sync `signedIn` (`packages/contracts/src/api/sync.ts:5,65`), and the real Sync acts on the Session's Account. In the fake, after `account.endSessions()`, `account.me` answers 401 but `sync.push` succeeds. `sync.pull` with no token at all returns every record.
- Consequence: Core tests of "Session ended elsewhere → Sync stops and the reader is signed out" pass only when they add `failNext('unauthorized')` by hand. A regression where the Core syncs with a dead or missing token stays green.
- Fix: call `sessionOf(context)` in both Sync procedures, after `arrive`, as `account.me` does. Key `records` by the Account's email if the fake is to support several Accounts.

## a1.9 Fake backend keeps Sync records after `account.delete`

- Status: CONFIRMED (reproduced: 2 records left after delete)
- Severity: medium (test fidelity, privacy path)
- Where: `packages/core/src/fakes/fake-backend.ts:365-371`, with `records` and `sequence` at `:225-226`
- Defect: the real `user_records.account_id` references `accounts.id` with `onDelete: 'cascade'` (`apps/backend/src/database/schema.ts:215-217`), so deleting an Account removes its records. The fake deletes the Account and its Sessions but keeps `records`. Because records are not keyed by Account, they also leak to any Account signed in later.
- Consequence: a test of "delete Account, then sign in again with the same email" pulls the old user state back. This hides bugs in the delete-account flow, where the device should start empty.
- Fix: clear `records` and reset `sequence` in `delete`, or better, key records per Account (see F1) and drop that Account's map.

## a1.10 Fake Sign-in code: the fifth wrong try breaks the contract

- Status: CONFIRMED (reproduced)
- Severity: low
- Where: `packages/core/src/fakes/fake-backend.ts:324-334`
- Defect: on the fifth wrong code the fake throws `WRONG_CODE` with `triesLeft: 0`, and the next try gets `CODE_EXPIRED`. The contract requires `triesLeft` to be positive (`packages/contracts/src/api/account.ts:73`). The real backend answers `TOO_MANY_REQUESTS` without `Retry-After` once every try is spent (`apps/backend/src/account/authenticator.ts:271,291-292`, `account-procedures.ts:56-57`).
- Consequence: the Core's handling of spent tries is never tested against the real answer, and it shows «0 tries left» in place of the too-many-requests state.
- Fix: when `triesLeft` reaches 0, delete the code and throw `refusal('TOO_MANY_REQUESTS', context)`. Answer `TOO_MANY_REQUESTS` for later tries until a new code is requested.

## a1.11 Fake Sign-in code does not normalise the email

- Status: CONFIRMED (traced)
- Severity: low (the Core normalises before calling, so nothing breaks today)
- Where: `packages/core/src/fakes/fake-backend.ts:317-333`
- Defect: `requestCode` and `verifyCode` key codes and Accounts by the raw `input.email` and answer it raw. The contract says the answer's email is "trimmed and lower-cased" (`packages/contracts/src/api/account.ts:24`), and the backend applies `AccountEmailSchema`. The fake's Google and Apple paths do lower-case.
- Consequence: a Core change that stops normalising would pass in the fake and fail in the real backend.
- Fix: run `AccountEmailSchema.parse(input.email)` in both procedures before using the email.

## a1.12 Memory content store never clears «needs new recording»

- Status: CONFIRMED (traced)
- Severity: low (test fidelity)
- Where: `packages/core/src/fakes/memory-content-store.ts:19,81`
- Defect: `needsNewRecording` is fixed when the store is created. The real `applyPatch` clears the mark for every Word that gets a live Recording in a Patch (ADR 0099; `packages/language-pack/src/apply-patch.ts:97-103`). The Core reads it at `packages/core/src/media/media.ts:142,195`.
- Consequence: a Core test of "a new Recording arrives by Patch, so pronunciation comes back" cannot be written against the fake, or gives the wrong answer.
- Fix: keep a mutable `Set` of marked Words and, in `take`, delete the `wordId` of every live recording row of an applied Patch.
